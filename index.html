Questions

The things everyone asks before buying.

Do I need coding experience?

No. If you can download a file and talk to Muse, you can do this. Download the kit, tell your Muse where it is, and Muse runs the checks and does the technical steps for you. You only approve the two things that are yours: creating your own free Enable Banking account, and approving access in your own Revolut app on your phone. Muse never sees your bank password or private key.

Is this affiliated with Revolut or Enable Banking?

No. It's an independent connector built on Enable Banking's public open-banking platform. Revolut and Enable Banking are their owners' trademarks, and you'll hold your own accounts with both.

Can it move my money or make payments?

No. The consent you grant is read-only account information — balances and transactions. There is no payment capability in the connector or the consent.

Why do I need my own Enable Banking account?

That's how open banking works: access is granted to you, for your accounts, in your name — so you create your own free Enable Banking account during setup, and Muse walks you through it. It's free for your own accounts, and it means your details and bank data never pass through anyone else.

Which countries are covered?

Revolut accounts across the EU and EEA, where Enable Banking provides access under PSD2.

Which accounts does it see?

All the Revolut accounts covered by your consent — typically your Main and Pro accounts, savings vaults, and currency accounts — with balances and transaction history for each.

Does it work with other banks?

Yes, as an expansion. The same Enable Banking feed covers many other European banks, and the Multi-Bank Pack extends the connector to them. Revolut is the proven, headline setup — for other banks we check coverage for your specific bank before you commit.

I'm not technical. Which package should I pick?

Any of them — no coding is needed with any pack, because your Muse does the setup. If you'd like a person alongside you as well, pick Done-For-You: we go through your account setup and phone approval with you in one guided session and don't finish until Muse is showing your live balances.

Does my approval expire?

Bank approvals under open banking are time-limited (typically up to 180 days) and need renewing now and then. Muse walks you through renewing in a couple of minutes, and the Care Plan includes help with it.

What do I need before I start?

A Revolut account held in the EU/EEA, a phone with the Revolut app for approving access, and Muse. Your free Enable Banking account is created during setup — Muse walks you through it. That's it, and no coding experience.
